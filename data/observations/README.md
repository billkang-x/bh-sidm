# Stage-5 LRD Target Envelope

The stage-5 feasibility maps use three black-hole target masses:

```text
1e5, 1e6, 1e7 M_sun
```

They are deliberately a bracket, not one exact LRD mass. Broad-line virial
mass estimates are model dependent because electron scattering, obscuration,
and non-standard bolometric corrections can substantially lower the inferred
mass.

Primary sources used to define the bracket:

- *Little red dots as young supermassive black holes in dense
  ionized cocoons*, arXiv:2503.16595v4. Electron-scattering line models give
  `M_BH = 1e5-1e7 M_sun`.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16595
- *What you see is what you get: empirically measured bolometric luminosities
  of Little Red Dots*, arXiv:2509.05434v1. Empirical bolometric corrections
  give an indicative `M_BH = 1e5-1e7 M_sun` and galaxy scale near `1e8 M_sun`.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.05434
- *Black Hole Stars Across the Universe*,
  arXiv:2606.17271v1. Eddington conversion of the selected luminosity range
  spans roughly `1e4-1e7 M_sun`.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17271
- *Dark Bondi Accretion Aided by Baryons and the Origin
  of JWST Little Red Dots*, arXiv:2506.17641v1. This is the direct comparison
  model: a `1e9 M_sun` halo reaches a `1e7 M_sun` black hole within `500 Myr`
  at `z = 4-11`.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17641

The project therefore reports all three target thresholds and does not treat
the legacy `1e6-1e8 M_sun` interval as a measurement without qualification.
